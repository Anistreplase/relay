Core operational workflows

Step-by-step reference for maintaining vulnerable resident records before, during, and after hazard events.

1. Household registration & duplicate check Register households with head details, purok, address, vulnerable members, and assistance needs. New records save as Unverified and enter the validation queue. Barangay accounts auto-assign their barangay; City-Wide Admin must select a barangay.
2. Record verification Review submitted or updated household records and mark them Verified, Needs Correction, or Unable to Verify with remarks. Only Verified records reach city responders and partner offices.
3. Offline preparedness & evacuation tracking Download verified records and area maps while connected, use the priority list for outreach planning, and update household evacuation statuses—queuing changes locally if connectivity drops.
4. Post-event revalidation & reports Follow due and overdue revalidation reminders after an event to update resident conditions or transfers, and export permitted summaries (vulnerable residents per purok, priority list, evacuation status, or pending validation) as PDF or CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about access, record visibility, and offline behavior.

Why can I only see records from one barangay?

Barangay-scoped accounts are restricted to their assigned barangay workspace (San Miguel, Tibanga, or Saray). City-Wide Administration accounts can switch workspaces or view across barangays.

When do city responders and partner offices see a new record?

A record must be reviewed and marked Verified first. Unverified entries and records needing correction remain in the validation workflow.

What happens when internet service is lost during field updates?

Relay enters Offline Mode so you can continue navigating and reviewing saved data. Queued updates stay on the device and synchronize once a connection returns.

System scope & boundaries

Information management only: Relay manages vulnerability and readiness records. It does not conduct rescue or evacuation operations, issue emergency warnings, diagnose medical conditions/disabilities, or distribute relief goods.

Official intake forms: Evacuee intake on national forms (such as CSWDO intake) remains the responsibility of the designated office.
